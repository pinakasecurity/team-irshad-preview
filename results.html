Process & Results

From appraisal to sold

Our process is straightforward: we give you real numbers, real comparable sales, and zero pressure. Here’s how it works, and what vendors say about it.

The Process

Four steps to your number

No guesswork. No suburb-wide averages. Just real, street-level data specific to your property.

01
Day 0 · 5 minutes

Book a time

Pick a slot online, or call/text directly. No forms to chase, no waiting on a callback.

  • Evenings & weekends available
  • Confirmed same day
02
Day 1–2 · 30–40 min on-site

We walk the property

A full walkthrough covering condition, layout, and anything that adds or costs value, not a drive-by guess.

  • Land size & zoning check
  • Renovation/repair impact noted
  • Layout flow assessed
03
Day 2–3 · Delivered in writing

We build your report

Real, recent comparable sales for your street, not suburb-wide averages, mapped against your property specifically.

  • 3–5 direct street comparables
  • Price range, not a single guess
  • Market trend context
04
Your timeline

You decide, no pressure

Sell now, wait, or just file it away. Plenty of people book an appraisal purely to know where they stand.

  • Zero obligation to list
  • Follow-up only if you want it
  • Report is yours to keep

Questions

Before you book

Does an appraisal cost anything?

No. It’s free and there’s no obligation to list your property with us afterward.

How long does it take?

Most appraisals are done within 30–40 minutes on-site, with your written report following within a day.

Do I have to sell?

Not at all. Plenty of people book an appraisal just to know where they stand.

Which suburbs do you cover?

Flat Bush, Ormiston, Goodwood Heights, Beachlands, Papakura, East Tamaki, Botany, Totara Heights, Clover Park, The Gardens and Hillpark.

What’s in the report?

Your report includes 3–5 comparable sales from your street or nearby, a realistic price range based on current market conditions, and notes on anything affecting your property’s value specifically.
